Hear the HOWLs Inside My Head

Formerly Howl of Madness. A world premiere reading, part of The Lab Series at The Ohio State University Department of Theatre.

The play follows Allen Ginsberg as his poetry and his life become entangled: his sexuality, his sanity, his desire to love, and his drive to make work that lasts.

The Eastward Journey of A Western Girl

A full-length comedy/drama, approximately 70 minutes.

Valerie moved to New York City to make herself into an artist, but the desert-born Nevada girl is torn between her duties to family back home, and her desire to make herself into the woman she always wanted to be.
