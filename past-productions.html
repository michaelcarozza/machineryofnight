Weight

A full-length drama. Atlas is damned to hold up the earth on his shoulders for all of eternity and taunted by Heracles, while a young girl in Britain struggles with her homosexuality and the burden of her secret. Both must carry the weight.

Hear the HOWLs Inside My Head

Formerly Howl of Madness. A staged reading presented by Machinery of Night Co. as part of The Lab Series at The Ohio State University Department of Theatre.

The play follows Allen Ginsberg as his poetry and his life become entangled: his sexuality, his sanity, his desire to love, and his drive to make work that lasts.

Written and directed by
Michael Ferdinando Carozza

The Eastward Journey of A Western Girl

A full-length comedy/drama, approximately 70 minutes.

Valerie moved to New York City to make herself into an artist, but the desert-born Nevada girl is torn between her duties to her family back home and her desire to take the next step and become the woman she always wanted to be.

Written and directed by
Michael Ferdinando Carozza

IN TRANSIT: A Series of Short Car Plays

For a Muse of Fire

A 10-minute drama, staged inside a car with the audience, as part of IN TRANSIT: A Series of Short Car Plays, with The Ohio State University.

A blind woman gets into a taxi just trying to get home, and finds in her driver, tormented by his past, something that can heal him.

Written by
Michael Ferdinando Carozza
2014 January 2014
24 Hour Theatre Festival, The Ohio State University Department of Theatre

A Film for Two

A 10-minute comedy, formally titled The Movie. Presented in the 24 Hour Theatre Festival at The Ohio State University Department of Theatre, produced by Alpha Psi Omega.

Two friends on a Friday night try to focus on a movie, until they are interrupted by the drama of another friend who knows too much.

Written by
Michael Ferdinando Carozza
