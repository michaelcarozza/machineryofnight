About

Machinery of Night Co. is a company of artists, producers, and designers. We make new work and reimagine existing pieces, and we aim for immersive, imaginative experiences that stay with audiences.

Our mission is to bring fearless imagination to every stage we work on.

The company has developed new plays through readings, presented a full production of the musical Spring Awakening in partnership with The Lab Series at The Ohio State University Department of Theatre, and staged short plays in unusual settings, including inside a car.

Our process

  1. Start with the story

    We begin with a central question and dig into the material to find its core.

  2. Build together

    Writers, directors, designers, and performers collaborate from the start and share creative ownership.

  3. Push the form

    We ask what theatre can do that other art forms cannot, and use staging choices to surprise.

  4. Make it land

    We bring precision to every detail so the production works fully in front of an audience.
